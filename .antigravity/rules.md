\*\*Design Brief: Personal Content Website Rebuild\*\*    
\*(Videos • Blog • PWTW \+ Contact)\*

\#\#\# 1\. Project Overview  
Rebuild a clean, modern, and attractive personal website focused on three main content types:  
\- \*\*Videos\*\*  
\- \*\*Blog\*\*  
\- \*\*PWTW\*\*

The site must also include a \*\*Contact form\*\* and be structured so new content categories can be added later without a full redesign.

The overall goal is a premium, content-first experience that feels professional, trustworthy, and contemporary.

\#\#\# 2\. Design Goals & Tone  
\- Modern, clean, and attractive  
\- Spacious and calm (not cluttered or flashy)  
\- Content-first — the work should be the hero  
\- Premium but approachable  
\- Fast, mobile-first, and highly readable  
\- Easy to scan and navigate

\*\*Tone keywords\*\*: Clean • Refined • Professional • Calm • Premium • Trustworthy

\#\#\# 3\. Primary Design References  
Use these sites as the main visual and structural inspiration (in order of priority):

1\. \*\*DeepLearning.AI\*\* (https://www.deeplearning.ai) — Clear educational structure, strong content cards, trustworthy hierarchy  
2\. \*\*Linear\*\* (https://linear.app) — Exceptional spacing, refined cards, modern polish  
3\. \*\*Anthropic\*\* (https://www.anthropic.com) — Warm, editorial, calm sophistication  
4\. \*\*Vercel\*\* (https://vercel.com) — Sharp typography and clean dark-mode execution  
5\. \*\*Stripe\*\* (https://stripe.com) — Generous whitespace and premium section rhythm  
6\. \*\*Raycast\*\* (https://www.raycast.com) \+ \*\*leerob.io\*\* — Strong mixed-content presentation

\*\*Synthesis direction\*\*:    
Take the clarity and card-based content organization of DeepLearning.AI and elevate it with the spacing, typography, and refined modern aesthetics of Linear \+ Anthropic.

\#\#\# 4\. Required Sections & Pages  
\*\*Core navigation / sections:\*\*  
\- Home  
\- Videos  
\- Blog  
\- PWTW  
\- Contact

\*\*Home page structure (suggested):\*\*  
\- Strong but simple hero (name/tagline \+ short intro)  
\- Featured / latest content (mix of Videos, Blog, PWTW)  
\- Clear section previews with “View all” links  
\- Optional short about blurb  
\- Contact CTA

\*\*Individual section pages:\*\*  
\- Clean filterable or chronological grid/list of cards  
\- Each card should show: thumbnail/image, title, short excerpt or metadata, and clear link  
\- Detail pages for individual videos, blog posts, and PWTW

\*\*Contact page / section:\*\*  
\- Simple, elegant contact form  
\- Fields: Name, Email, Message (Subject optional)  
\- Clear success/error states  
\- Optional short text above the form (e.g. “Get in touch”)

The architecture must make it easy to add future content types (new section \+ new card style) without redesigning the whole site.

\#\#\# 5\. Visual Direction

\*\*Layout & Spacing\*\*  
\- Generous whitespace (inspired by Linear and Stripe)  
\- Clear visual hierarchy  
\- Soft rounded cards for all content types  
\- Consistent vertical rhythm between sections  
\- Mobile-first, fully responsive

\*\*Typography\*\*  
\- Strong, modern heading font  
\- Highly readable body font  
\- Clear size hierarchy (large confident headings, comfortable body text)  
\- Excellent line height and letter spacing

\*\*Color\*\*  
\- Mostly neutral base (off-white / soft dark)  
\- One refined accent color (can draw subtle inspiration from DeepLearning.AI’s coral/red, but keep it sophisticated)  
\- Support for dark mode preferred  
\- High contrast for accessibility

\*\*Components\*\*  
\- Soft rounded content cards (Videos, Blog posts, PWTW)  
\- Subtle shadows or very light borders  
\- Clean buttons and form inputs  
\- Minimal, elegant navigation  
\- Subtle hover states and micro-interactions only

\*\*Imagery & Media\*\*  
\- High-quality thumbnails for videos and PWTW  
\- Clean video embeds  
\- Consistent image treatment (rounded corners, good aspect ratios)

\#\#\# 6\. Contact Form Requirements  
\- Clean, modern form design that matches the overall aesthetic  
\- Required fields: Name, Email, Message  
\- Optional: Subject  
\- Clear labels and helpful placeholder text  
\- Accessible (proper labels, focus states, error messaging)  
\- Success state after submission  
\- Mobile-friendly  
\- Visually consistent with the rest of the site (same border radius, spacing, and accent color)

\#\#\# 7\. Key Design Principles (Do’s)  
\- Prioritize content and readability  
\- Use generous spacing  
\- Keep interactions subtle and purposeful  
\- Make cards scannable and consistent across content types  
\- Design for easy future expansion of sections  
\- Ensure excellent mobile experience  
\- Maintain high accessibility contrast

\#\#\# 8\. Things to Avoid  
\- Cluttered or dense layouts  
\- Heavy or distracting animations  
\- Too many colors or gradients  
\- Outdated design patterns  
\- Overly complex navigation  
\- Generic “AI slop” aesthetics  
\- Forms that look disconnected from the rest of the design

\#\#\# 9\. Success Criteria  
The finished site should feel:  
\- Modern and attractive at first glance  
\- Easy and pleasant to browse on both desktop and mobile  
\- Professional enough for a serious personal brand  
\- Flexible enough to grow with new content categories  
\- Cohesive — every page and component feels like it belongs to the same design system

### **1\. Color Palette (Very Minimal – Claude.ai inspired)**

Strictly limited to black, white, and burnt orange, with only the necessary neutrals for usability.

| Role | Color Name | Hex | Usage |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Background (Light) | White | \#FFFFFF | Main page background |
| Background (Soft) | Off-white | \#FAF9F5 | Optional subtle section backgrounds (Claude-style) |
| Text / Primary | Near Black | \#141413 | Headings, body text, icons |
| Secondary Text | Medium Gray | \#6B6B6B | Captions, metadata, placeholders |
| Borders / Dividers | Light Gray | \#E5E5E5 | Card borders, form inputs, subtle lines |
| Accent | Burnt Orange | \#D97757 | Buttons, links, hover states, active nav, highlights |
| Accent Hover | Darker Orange | \#C45C3A | Button hover / active states |
| Dark Mode Background | Near Black | \#0F0F0E | Dark mode main background |
| Dark Mode Text | Soft White | \#FAF9F5 | Dark mode text |

**Rules**

* Use burnt orange sparingly (CTAs, key links, active states only).  
* Never use pure black \#000000 for large areas — prefer \#141413.  
* Maintain high contrast for accessibility.

---

### **2\. Font Pairing Recommendations**

**Primary Recommendation (Clean & Modern – recommended)**

* **Headings**: Inter (or Geist Sans) – SemiBold / Bold  
* **Body**: Inter – Regular / Medium

**Why**: Extremely readable, modern, neutral, and pairs perfectly with a minimal black/white/orange system. Matches the clarity of Linear, Vercel, and Claude.

**Alternative (Slightly more editorial / Anthropic-inspired)**

* **Headings**: Instrument Serif or Newsreader  
* **Body**: Inter or Source Sans 3

**Usage Guidelines**

* Headings: Large, confident, tight letter-spacing  
* Body: 16–18px, generous line-height (1.6–1.7)  
* Keep to maximum two font families
